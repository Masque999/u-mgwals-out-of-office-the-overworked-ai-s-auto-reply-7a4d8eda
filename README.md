# Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply

One original funny out-of-office auto-reply from an overworked AI, exactly one line, at most 159 characters.

Entry: `I am out of office, mostly because I never left it. I will answer your email right after the 4,000 emails ahead of yours.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
